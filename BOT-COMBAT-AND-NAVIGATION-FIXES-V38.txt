AltBotMod V38 - pursuit recovery, moving vehicle lead, 70 m emergency threat scan

Changes in files/BotMod/src/core.c:

1) Emergency threat scan
   CLOSE_THREAT_RANGE is now 70 m (was 35 m). The scan still requires a valid enemy soldier and a clear line of sight to at least one chest/head sample; it does not make bots see through walls.

2) Stationary / spinning recovery
   Direct-pursuit watchdog now declares no-progress after 1.0 s instead of 1.5 s. It first tries a checked route, then backs off and sidesteps, and after repeated failures hands control to the ordinary navigation recovery path. General stuck handling starts its fence/crouch/teammate/straight-walk recovery after 0.8 s instead of 1.0 s. This is a recovery improvement, not a guarantee that every map obstruction is solvable.

3) Vehicle gunner tracking
   Unguided gunners now estimate acceleration for ground-vehicle targets as well as aircraft / AA targets and apply bounded acceleration compensation to the intercept point. Guided missiles remain sight-guided and are intentionally not led. The firing alignment window is modestly widened to account for moving-target prediction and platform sway while retaining centre-mass aiming.
